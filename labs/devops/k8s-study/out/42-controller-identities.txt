## 4. deployment-controller 와 replicaset-controller 의 규칙 (파드·ReplicaSet 관련만)
  [system:controller:deployment-controller]
    deployments                                   get,list,update,watch
    deployments/status                            update
    deployments/finalizers                        update
    replicasets                                   create,delete,get,list,patch,update,watch
    pods                                          get,list,update,watch
  [system:controller:replicaset-controller]
    replicasets                                   get,list,update,watch
    replicasets/status                            update
    replicasets/finalizers                        update
    pods                                          create,delete,list,patch,watch

## 5. kubectl auth can-i 로 본 권한 차이 (--as 로 ServiceAccount 흉내, 읽기 전용 검사)
  deployment-controller  create pods: no
  deployment-controller  update replicasets.apps: yes
  deployment-controller  update deployments.apps: yes
  replicaset-controller  create pods: yes
  replicaset-controller  update replicasets.apps: yes
  replicaset-controller  update deployments.apps: no
